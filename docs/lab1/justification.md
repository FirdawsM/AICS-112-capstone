# Why containment needs human approval

I require human approval before containment because isolating a host or disabling an account can stop real business work. The data here is synthetic, but in a real microfinance setting a wrongly isolated finance laptop could block payment processing, and a wrongly disabled privileged account could lock out the people who need to respond. Automated scoring can be wrong, since the model uses weights from a small dataset, and an AI summary can hallucinate or be steered by prompt injection in event text. A human analyst can check what the pipeline cannot see, such as travel, a change window, or a running batch job. Low impact steps like creating a case or notifying an analyst are reversible, so they run automatically. High impact steps wait for a named decision, and every decision is written to a hash-chained audit log for later review.
